How to Play Pik

Goal: Stretch your line as far as possible over 8 rounds by naming rare, niche, or obscure answers.

Rarity Tiers:

  • Common: +110px
  • Uncommon: +180px
  • Rare: +270px
  • Epic: +390px
  • The Pik (Gold): +540px

No Repeats: Each answer can only be used once per game.

Autocorrect: Minor typos will trigger a confirmation prompt before submitting.

Pik-ed Battle Lobby

How Pik-ed Works:

  • Up to 4 players compete on stacked live track lines.
  • Everyone gets 35 seconds to answer each question.
  • The player with the lowest rarity answer is eliminated!
  • If there is a tie for lowest rarity, the player who took the longest time to answer is eliminated.
  • The last player standing wins!
