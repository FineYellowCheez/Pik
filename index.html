How to Play Pik

1. The Objective

Each run features 8 prompts across different categories. Type a valid answer that genuinely fits the prompt—instead of guessing the most obvious answer, aim for niche, obscure, or deep-cut answers. The rarer your pick, the farther your line stretches across the track!

2. Rarity Tiers & Stretch 5 px = 1 pt
Common — Obvious, everyday first thought
110 px
Uncommon — Solid pick, slightly off the beaten path
180 px
Sarcastic — Clever, pedantic, or technically-true loophole
225 px
Rare — Specific, clever, or specialized pick
270 px
Epic — Deep-cut knowledge most people miss
390 px
The Pik — Hyper-niche, extraordinary deep pull
540 px
3. Answer Rules
  • • Be Specific & Accurate: Your answer must genuinely match the prompt. Overly broad or vague guesses will be rejected so you can try again.
  • • Spelling Forgiveness: Minor typos and capitalization differences are automatically recognized without penalizing you.
  • • No Duplicate Answers: Each answer can only be used once per 8-question run.
  • • Skipping: If you're stumped, press Skip to advance—it adds 0 px to your line.
4. Game Modes
Standard

Untimed 8-question run. Take your time to dig deep.

Timed

30 seconds per question before it auto-skips.

Daily

One shared 8-question set every day for all players.

Post-Game Breakdown: After completing all 8 questions, use the Inspect Line slider to pan across your full track and click any question card on the left to view its rarity analysis and rarer examples.
